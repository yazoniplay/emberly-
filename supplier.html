Launch checklist

Product photos and image rights

The gallery uses supplier-hosted images that appear to depict the listed fireplace-style diffuser. Confirm permission to use these images commercially and confirm the images match the exact variant being sold.

Specifications and included accessories

Confirm capacity, lighting modes, timer, power connector and input, essential-oil compatibility, included cable, manual, safety warnings, and whether the selected variant includes a remote. Supplier listing attributes may vary or conflict.

Fulfilment and policies

Confirm destination countries, stock, fulfilment cost, delivery estimates, tracking, returns, damaged-item handling, customs and tax wording, and a monitored customer-support email.

Payment and legal readiness

Checkout is not configured. Before launch, an adult parent or legal guardian should handle payment-provider account ownership, verification, business obligations and required registrations. Use secure server-side checkout and verify payments server-side.

The 899 SEK price is a test assumption. Do not advertise unverified features, certifications, stock, delivery promises or customer reviews.
