We'd rather tell you what's confirmed and what still needs checking than make promises we can't support.

What is Emberly?

Emberly is a home-ambience concept centred on a fireplace-inspired diffuser. The supplier describes a simulated flame effect created with light and mist; it is not a real fire or a heater.

What are the exact specifications?

The supplier listing contains claims about capacity, lighting and timer functions, but details can vary by variant and the listing is inconsistent in places. Confirm the exact purchasable SKU, manual, power requirements and included accessories before treating any specification as final.

Can I add essential oils?

Do not add essential oils unless the instructions for the exact selected model explicitly permit it. Compatibility and safe-use directions must be confirmed with the supplier.

Where do you ship, and how long does it take?

Shipping destinations, costs and delivery estimates have not been configured. We cannot promise worldwide shipping or a delivery date until the supplier route has been checked for the destination. See the shipping policy template.

What is the return policy?

The final return and damaged-item process must be confirmed and published before launch. See the returns policy template.

Are the reviews genuine?

There are no customer reviews published yet. We will not invent testimonials, ratings or verified-purchase labels.

Can I check out now?

No. The shopping bag is a demo and checkout is not connected to a payment provider. No payment will be taken and adding an item to the bag does not place an order.

How can I contact support?

A verified support email has not been configured yet. Customer support details must be added before the store accepts orders.

Before launch, verify the exact product variant, product image permissions, specifications, fulfilment options, shipping costs and customer policies.
